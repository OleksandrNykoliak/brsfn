01 · Name

Our Name

Boristene takes its name from Borysthenes — the name ancient Greek geographers, including Herodotus, gave to the Dnipro, the river at the heart of Ukraine. Like the river it is named after, the association sees itself as a channel carrying Ukrainian culture, memory and voice into Italy and Europe.

02 · Story

Our Story

Boristene was founded in Milan in 2022, at the start of Russia's full-scale invasion, by Svitlana Tereshchenko, out of love for Ukraine and freedom. In 2024 the association was formally registered under Italian law as Boristene ETS. Since then it has grown into an organisation producing exhibitions, festivals, concerts, conferences and immersive multimedia projects across Italy and on international stages.

03 · Mission

Mission

To engage the Italian public, counter distorted information and show the real Ukraine — its history, values and cultural richness — through cultural, educational and artistic projects.

04 · Vision

Vision

By sharing Ukrainian creativity and ideas, we foster collaboration and understanding between peoples and work toward a future in which Ukraine stands alongside its European partners to safeguard shared values.

From Milan to international stages, Boristene creates spaces where Ukrainian culture can be discovered, understood and experienced.
